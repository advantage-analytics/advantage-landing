Direction B — Court-first

Promotes today's Shots tab to the whole page. Filters on the left pose a question, the court answers it, and every number on the right is a readout of what is currently drawn — select a cluster and the clips for exactly those points fill the readout beside it. Spatial truths land in a second, and it looks like nothing else a club coach has access to.

Tradeoff: everything non-spatial — rally length, momentum, error counts, the head-to-head comparison — loses its home and has to go somewhere else. A first-time visitor has to work the filters before the page tells them anything.
