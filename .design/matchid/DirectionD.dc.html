Direction D — The ledger

Everything on one screen, no tabs and no rail: identity collapses to a scoreboard bar, the game-by-game spine runs across the top, and three ledger columns carry the full comparison beneath it. Nothing is more than one glance away, printing and PDF export are near-free, and it respects the analyst who already knows what they are looking for.

Tradeoff: the densest and the coldest — it makes no argument and gives a first-time club player no way in. Adding a metric means finding space rather than adding a row, and it is the hardest of the four to collapse onto a phone.
