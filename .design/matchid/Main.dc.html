Direction A — Lede first

The report states its finding before it shows a number: one sentence, the two figures that prove it, and the clips that back it, all above the fold. The three tabs become chapters in one scroll, so a claim, the number under it and the clip that proves it sit together instead of one tab apart. Makes the Advantage Intelligence summary the product rather than a rail footnote, and reads the way a coach actually talks.

Tradeoff: it is only as good as the generated insight — a thin or wrong lede damages the whole page, and every match must produce one. Analysts who want the raw comparison scroll past the argument to get it.
